## Target role

Senior Manager, Director, or Principal-level Cloud Operations, Platform
Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, or Cloud Infrastructure
leadership role.

## Location and work arrangement

Remote within the United States, preferably compatible with Pacific Time, or
onsite or hybrid in the Portland or Beaverton, Oregon area. The role should not
require relocation outside that area. US work authorization is required.

## Profile

Cloud operations and platform leader with 20+ years in technology, including
12+ across DevOps, SRE, and cloud infrastructure and nine years in payments
platform operations. Has led small engineering teams for over six years and
combines people leadership with strong technical judgment and practical
operational ownership.

## Responsibilities

- Lead cloud operations, platform engineering, SRE, DevOps, or infrastructure
  teams.
- Set technical direction for secure, reliable, scalable production platforms.
- Guide AWS multi-account architecture, cloud governance, service selection,
  cost management, and Well-Architected practices, with useful GCP experience.
- Improve reliability through incident management, root cause analysis,
  disaster recovery, observability, release coordination, and reduction of
  operational toil.
- Design and maintain Terraform infrastructure as code and production CI/CD,
  especially with GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Docker, and automation tools.
- Partner with security and compliance teams on IAM, AWS Security Hub, SOC 2,
  ISO 27001, PCI DSS, and practical production controls.
- Coach engineers, improve engineering standards, and coordinate delivery
  through Agile or Scrum practices.
- Use GenAI and agent tooling pragmatically to improve incident triage,
  operations, developer workflows, and team effectiveness, including
  MCP-connected tools and model-assisted automation.
- Work closely with engineering, security, product, and executive stakeholders
  on architecture and operational decisions.

## Must-have qualifications

- Senior technical leadership in cloud operations, platform engineering, SRE,
  DevOps, or cloud infrastructure.
- Demonstrated people leadership and team management experience.
- Strong AWS experience, including multi-account Organizations, IAM, Identity
  Center, Security Hub, and the AWS Well-Architected Framework.
- Terraform and infrastructure-as-code experience.
- Production reliability, incident response, observability, and operational
  excellence.
- Production CI/CD and automation experience.
- Ability to lead architecture and execution without writing production
  application code every day.

## Nice-to-have qualifications

- GCP, Docker, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Ansible, Puppet, Bash, Grafana,
  Datadog, or Splunk experience.
- SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, or similar security and compliance experience.
- AWS Certified Solutions Architect or comparable cloud certification.
- Practical AI-assisted operations or developer tooling experience.
- Experience supporting high-scale consumer, payments, SaaS, or internet
  platforms.

## Company and role fit

Prefer a product or technology company with a meaningful cloud platform,
serious reliability and security expectations, and enough maturity to value
thoughtful governance. The role should emphasize platform outcomes,
architecture, reliability, and team leadership rather than quota-carrying
presales, customer implementation services, or heavy daily software
development.

## Compensation

Prefer roles with stated total compensation or salary of at least $150,000
annually, with $180,000 or more preferred. Roles without a stated range remain
eligible when the role fit is otherwise strong.

## Exclusions

Exclude roles requiring active security clearance, hardware or manufacturing
work, quota or presales ownership, professional-services delivery, or a primary
identity as software development engineer. Avoid roles whose core expectation
is hands-on coding every day, or whose core expectation is running container
orchestration platforms day to day.
